About us

Where style and sophistication come together

Lelo's Hair Boutique is a private garden salon in Parklands, Christchurch. It's a different pace from a busy high-street salon. One chair, one stylist, and an appointment that's entirely about you.

From everyday cuts to complete colour transformations and wedding-day styling, every visit ends the same way: walking out feeling confident and transformed.
