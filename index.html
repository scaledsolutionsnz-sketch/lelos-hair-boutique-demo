What she does

Six services, one stylist. If you are not sure which one you need, ring and describe it. That conversation is free and usually sorts it out.

Hair colouring

Full colour, foils, balayage and creative tones, mixed to suit your skin rather than a swatch book. Corrective work taken on case by case.

Wedding & occasion

Bridal and event styling for you or the whole party, done on site at the studio on the morning of the day.

Women's cut & style

Cuts shaped to your face, your hair type and how you actually wear it on a Tuesday morning, not just how it looks leaving the chair.

Treatments

Deep conditioning and repair for hair that has been coloured, heat styled or simply had a hard year.

Blow dry & finish

A proper blow wave that holds. Book it before an event, or just because it is Friday.

Men's cut

Tidy, sharp and unrushed, because there is only one chair to get through.

Pricing is not published here. Ring for a quote on colour, it depends on length, condition and what is already on the hair.

There is no second chair, so there is nobody else to hand you to.

Lelo's Hair Boutique is a private garden studio in Parklands, not a high street salon. One stylist, one appointment at a time. The person who talks through your colour is the person who mixes it, applies it and finishes it.

It is a slower way to run a salon and it is the entire point. Nothing is rushed to free the chair for the next booking, because there is no next booking sitting in the room with you.
