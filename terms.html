Appointments and bookings

Appointments are made by phone or email. Please give as much notice as you can if you need to change or cancel, so the time can be offered to another client.

Plans change and that is fine. We simply ask for reasonable notice for cancellations or reschedules. Repeated no shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

Prices are confirmed at consultation, because colour and styling work varies with hair length, condition and the result you are after. Any quote given before an appointment is an estimate until the consultation.
