Privacy Policy

Lelo's Hair Boutique · Parklands, Christchurch

This policy explains how Lelo's Hair Boutique handles personal information. This is placeholder content for the demo website and would be replaced with the salon's finalised policy before launch.

What we collect

When you contact the salon to book, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and notes about your appointment (for example your colour history) so we can look after your hair properly on return visits.

How it's used

Your details are used only to manage bookings, provide services and stay in touch about your appointments. We don't sell or share client information with third parties for marketing.

This website

This website doesn't use contact forms and doesn't store personal information itself. The call and email buttons simply open your own phone or email app. The embedded Google Map is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you can ask what personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted.

Contact

For any privacy questions, get in touch via the contact section on the main page.
