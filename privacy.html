Policy

Lelo’s Hair Boutique · Parklands, Christchurch

This policy explains what Lelo’s Hair Boutique does with the information you share. This is placeholder content for the demo website and would be replaced with the salon’s finalised policy before launch.

What we collect

When you contact the studio by phone or email we collect the details you choose to share, typically your name, contact information and what you would like done with your hair.

How it is used

Your details are used to arrange and carry out your appointment, and to get back in touch about it. They are not sold, rented or traded.

This website

This site does not run a booking system and does not ask you to create an account. The map on the home page is embedded from Google Maps, which applies its own privacy terms.

Photographs

Photographs of finished work are only shared publicly with the client’s agreement. If you would rather your hair was not photographed, say so at your appointment and that is the end of it.

Access and corrections

You can ask what information the studio holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, at any time. Details are in the visit section of the main page.
